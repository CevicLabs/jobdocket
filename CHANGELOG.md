# Release notes

## 1.5.2

First public release on Google Play.

- Jobs with client, status, notes and photos.
- Time logging per job, with a live timer.
- Parts and mileage per job.
- Calendar for planning the week.
- PDF job reports you can send any way you like.
- Backups you make yourself, with a reminder after 30 days without one.
- Free for your first 10 jobs and 3 reports, then a yearly subscription through Google Play.
