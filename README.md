# Job Docket

Your jobs, hours, parts and mileage, on your phone.

Job Docket is a job-sheet app for tradespeople and small service businesses on Android. Log the work as you do it, and send the client a clean PDF report when you're done.

This repository is for **support**: bug reports, feature requests and release notes. The app's source code is not public.

- About the app: <https://ceviclabs.com/jobdocket/>
- Privacy policy: <https://ceviclabs.com/jobdocket/privacy/>
- Release notes: [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md)

## What it does

- See your day at a glance: what's on, what's in progress, what's waiting.
- Keep every job with its client, status, notes and photos.
- Log your hours on each job as you work.
- Record parts and mileage against each job.
- Plan the week ahead in the calendar.
- Make a PDF job report and send it any way you like.

## Your data stays on your phone

Job Docket has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere. There's no account and no cloud. Your jobs live in a private database on your device, and you make backups yourself, to wherever you choose.
